Our Class Trip to Omu Resort

A day full of animals, adventure, and memories that will last a lifetime.

Written by

Victoria Costache

SSS 3  ·  Science

📍 Omu Resort, Lagos — Class of SSS 3

Our recent class trip to Omu resort was one of the most exciting experiences I've ever had. From the moment we arrived, everyone was full of energy and curiosity. The resort was lively, filled with nature, animals and adventure everywhere we turned.

The Animal Experience

We saw so many animals — lions, zebras, ostriches, eagles and even snakes. One of the most thrilling moments was when a cobra spat at us, but thankfully there was a glass protecting everyone. We also saw a snake shedding its skin, which was fascinating to watch.

While exploring the animal section, I discovered that a lion doesn't actually sound like what movies made us believe. When I first heard the roar I was expecting some other creature to have a loud yawn, but it turned out to be the roar of a lion — which surprised me greatly.

"It turned out to be the roar of a lion, which surprised me. Movies had not done it justice at all."
— Victoria Costache, SSS 3

Rides & Adventures

After exploring the animal section, we went for rides like roller coasters and other fun activities. There was a particular roller coaster that turned us at a full 360 degrees — it was quite terrifying but incredibly fun, although I doubt I will embark on that ride ever again. I missed kayaking this time, but it is definitely on my list for the next visit.

What We Learned

The trip was not only fun but also educational. I learned a lot about different animals and nature. I am really grateful for the experience and the memories I made with my classmates. It was a day full of laughter, adventure and discovery.

Tips & Advice

📖

Educational Tips for Students

  • Stay Organized: Use planners and checklists to keep track of assignments and deadlines.
  • Ask Questions: Don't hesitate to seek help from teachers or classmates when you're unsure.
  • Set Goals: Break big tasks into smaller, achievable goals and celebrate your progress.
  • Take Breaks: Balance study time with short breaks to stay focused and refreshed.
  • Read Widely: Explore books and resources beyond your textbooks to expand your knowledge.
  • Join Activities: Participate in clubs, sports, and events to develop new skills and friendships.
  • Practice Self-Care: Get enough sleep, eat healthy, and manage stress for better learning.
🤝

Advice for Parents & Students

  • Communicate Regularly: Parents and students should discuss school progress and challenges openly.
  • Encourage Curiosity: Support students in exploring their interests and asking questions.
  • Create a Study Space: Set up a quiet, comfortable area for homework and reading at home.
  • Celebrate Achievements: Recognize both big and small successes to boost confidence and motivation.
  • Foster Independence: Allow students to take responsibility for their learning and decisions.
  • Stay Involved: Attend school events, meetings, and stay informed about academic requirements.
  • Promote Balance: Help students balance academics with hobbies, rest, and social time.
